The most useful combination: Google’s components, NAnDoroid’s shaped controls, DMS/Dux’s modules, Caelestia’s cohesion, and Apple/GNOME’s overview-to-detail organization.

The design authority

Google’s design tactics

Contain related controls, prioritize a few interactions, and vary shape, type and color with purpose.

Expressive sliders

Five track sizes: 16, 24, 40, 56 and 96 dp. Compare S and M in our future mockups; retain immediate volume values.

Semantic color roles

Graphite surfaces, wallpaper-tinted containers and paired text colors. Consistent roles across every shortcut surface.

Spatial and effects motion

Geometry can use springs; opacity must not overshoot. Keep the current sidebar timings during the first visual pass.
